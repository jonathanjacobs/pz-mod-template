# Project compliance

This template is intended for work under The Indie Stone's current Project Zomboid Modding Policy and applicable distribution-platform rules.

Authoritative policy: <https://projectzomboid.com/blog/modding-policy/>

`docs/PZ_MODDING_POLICY.md` is the project's detailed engineering and release-control policy. Before the first public or Steam Workshop release, recheck the live policy and record its review date there.

This is an unofficial independent community-mod template; it does not imply affiliation with or endorsement by The Indie Stone.
